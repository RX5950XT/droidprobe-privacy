DroidProbe 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日

一句話

DroidProbe 不收集、不傳送、也不分享你的任何資料。App 沒有申請網路權限（INTERNET），技術上就無法把資料傳出這支手機。

權限與用途

下列權限只用來在手機上讀取硬體資訊或進行量測，結果只顯示在畫面上，不會上傳：

  • 位置：顯示 Wi-Fi 名稱、基地台、衛星與計算真北。只在 App 開著時使用。
  • 麥克風：噪音計與麥克風檢測。聲音不會錄成檔案。
  • 相機：讀取鏡頭規格與相機檢測。畫面不會存檔。
  • 電話狀態：顯示 SIM 卡資訊與行動網路類型。
  • 鄰近裝置（藍牙）：顯示藍牙名稱與已配對裝置數量。
  • 體能活動：計步器計算步數。
  • NFC、震動、生物辨識、Wi-Fi 狀態、音訊設定、高取樣率感測器：只在對應的工具或檢測中使用。

存在手機上的資料

量測紀錄、校準值與設定只存在 App 自己的儲存空間，不會備份到雲端。解除安裝 App 就會一起刪除。

分享與匯出

只有在你自己按下「分享」或「匯出」時，資料才會透過 Android 的分享選單交給你選擇的 App。之後的處理方式依該 App 的隱私權政策而定。

購買 Pro

Pro 透過 Google Play 結帳。付款資料由 Google 處理，適用 Google 隱私權政策；DroidProbe 拿不到你的付款資訊，只會從手機上的 Play 商店得知是否已購買。

第三方服務

DroidProbe 沒有廣告、沒有使用分析，也沒有當機回報服務。

兒童

DroidProbe 不是為 13 歲以下兒童設計的，也不會收集任何人的資料。

政策變更

如果政策有變，會更新這一頁並修改上方的生效日期。

聯絡方式

請使用 Google Play 商店頁面上的開發者電子郵件，或到 GitHub Issues 留言。

DroidProbe Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short

DroidProbe does not collect, transmit or share any of your data. The app does not request network access (INTERNET), so it technically cannot send anything off your phone.

Permissions and what they are used for

These permissions are used only to read hardware information or take measurements on the phone. Results are shown on screen and never uploaded:

  • Location: Wi-Fi name, cell towers, satellites and true north. Used only while the app is open.
  • Microphone: sound meter and microphone check. Audio is never saved to a file.
  • Camera: lens specifications and the camera check. Frames are never saved.
  • Phone state: SIM details and mobile network type.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): Bluetooth name and number of paired devices.
  • Physical activity: step counting in the pedometer.
  • NFC, vibration, biometrics, Wi-Fi state, audio settings and high-rate sensors: used only by the matching tool or check.

Data stored on your phone

Recordings, calibration values and settings are stored only in the app's private storage and are not backed up to the cloud. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Sharing and export

Data leaves the app only when you tap Share or Export yourself. It is then handed through the Android share sheet to the app you choose, whose own privacy policy applies.

Buying Pro

Pro is purchased through Google Play. Payment details are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. DroidProbe never sees your payment information; it only learns from the Play Store on your phone whether Pro has been bought.

Third-party services

DroidProbe has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Children

DroidProbe is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.

Contact
